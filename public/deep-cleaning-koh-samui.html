Questions

Deep cleaning FAQ

How much does a deep clean cost in Koh Samui?

Deep cleans start from ฿2,000 for a whole home. The final price depends on the size of the place and how much work it needs: very dirty homes, stubborn stains, a lot of glass or large homes take more time. We confirm the price with you before we start.

Can I get a price from photos?

Yes. When you book, choose Home cleaning, add the number of bedrooms and bathrooms and upload a few photos. We send you a rough estimate, and confirm the final price after we assess the place.

Do you clean homes that are very dirty?

Yes. We take on heavy cleans, including homes left full of rubbish with pests. See the before and after photos on this page.

Do I need to be there?

No. We can arrange a key safe, an access code or someone to let us in.

Do you bring cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes, our team brings its own cleaning products and equipment, included in the price.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Koh Samui, including Chaweng, Bophut, Lamai, Maenam, Bang Rak, Choeng Mon, Plai Laem, Nathon, Lipa Noi and Taling Ngam.
