How errands work

  • Tell us what you need, where to pick up and where to drop off. Add photos or a shopping list in the booking form.
  • Our admin team takes your request, confirms the details and price with you, and passes it to our driver team leader.
  • The team leader sends the driver nearest to your pickup point, so you don't pay for us to reach you.
  • Price: ฿200 service + ฿12 per km, counted from your pickup point to the drop-off, with a minimum of ฿300 per trip. Use the calculator above for an estimate.
  • Moving help is charged by the hour: ฿400 per hour.
  • You get updates and photos on LINE: on the way, picked up, delivered.
  • For items we buy for you, you pay the receipt amount plus the errand price.
  • Long waits or a trip cancelled after the driver has set off may cost ฿100–250.
