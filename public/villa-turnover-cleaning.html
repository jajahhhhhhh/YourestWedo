Every turnover

What we do between guests

  • Bedrooms: strip and make the beds, dust and vacuum
  • Bathrooms: clean showers, toilets, sinks and mirrors
  • Kitchen: wash up, wipe counters and appliances, empty the fridge of leftovers
  • Living areas and terraces: dust, vacuum and mop
  • Empty all bins and take the rubbish out
  • Fresh linen and laundry on request

How it works

Set it up once

  1. Send us your villa. Book online or message us with the address, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and your next check-out and check-in.
  2. We confirm the price. We assess the villa and agree a fixed price per turnover, plus access and where linen and supplies are kept.
  3. Send us your bookings. Tell us each new guest stay and we schedule the clean between check-out and check-in.
  4. Pay per clean. We send a payment link after each turnover; pay by PromptPay.

Questions from hosts

Villa turnover FAQ

Can you clean between a morning check-out and an afternoon check-in?

Yes. Send us your booking dates and check-out and check-in times, and we schedule the turnover clean in that gap. Booking early helps, especially on busy weekends.

How do your housekeepers get into the villa?

We arrange it with you in advance: a key safe or access code, keys left with your caretaker, or someone to let us in.

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Yes. Housekeepers bring their own cleaning equipment and products, included in the price.

Can you change the beds and wash the linen?

Yes, bed making with fresh linen and laundry are available on request. Tell us when you book, and where clean linen is kept.

How much does a villa turnover clean cost?

Starting prices are ฿1,000–1,500 for a 2-bedroom villa, ฿2,000 for 3 bedrooms and ฿3,000 for 4 bedrooms. We confirm the final price after seeing the villa, then it stays the same for every turnover unless the job changes.

Can you get the villa ready for arriving guests?

Yes. Besides cleaning, our team can buy and stock groceries before guests arrive, and pick guests up from Samui airport with our airport transfer service.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Koh Samui, including Chaweng, Bophut, Lamai, Maenam, Bang Rak, Choeng Mon, Plai Laem, Nathon, Lipa Noi and Taling Ngam.
