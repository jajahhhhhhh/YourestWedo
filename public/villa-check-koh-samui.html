Villa check FAQ

How much is a villa check?

฿600 per visit, anywhere on Koh Samui. Book a single visit or a regular schedule, for example every week or every two weeks while you're away.

What do you check?

We walk around the villa inside and out: doors, windows, locks and keys, signs of damage, leaks or pests. We run the taps, flush the toilets and open the windows to air the rooms, then send you photos.

How do I get the report?

We send photos and a short note by message (LINE or WhatsApp) after each visit, so you can see the villa for yourself.

Can you check the villa after a storm?

Yes. After heavy rain or a storm we can visit to look for leaks, fallen trees, power cuts or other damage and tell you what we find.

Can you let in tradespeople or check repair work?

Yes. We can open the villa for a technician or builder, check the work they've done and send you photos, and receive deliveries for you.

How do you get in?

We arrange access with you in advance: keys kept with us, a key safe or access code, or your caretaker.
