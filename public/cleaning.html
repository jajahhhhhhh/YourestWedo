Home Cleaning

A spotless home, without lifting a finger. Trained housekeepers come to villas, condos and homes all over Koh Samui, bring premium supplies and leave every room fresh. Book one visit, a regular schedule, or a turnover clean between guests.

Book a cleaning See prices

What's included

Everything taken care of

  • Kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas
  • Dusting, vacuuming, mopping and bin emptying
  • Bed making and linen change on request
  • Laundry and ironing on request
  • Turnover cleaning between guest check-out and check-in
  • Move-in, move-out and post-event cleans: see deep cleaning

Good to know

  • Housekeepers bring their own supplies and equipment.
  • Weekly, bi-weekly or one-off visits.
  • Secure key or access-code handover can be arranged in advance.
  • Hosting guests? See Airbnb & villa turnover cleaning.
