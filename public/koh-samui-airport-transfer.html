Questions

Airport transfer FAQ

How much is a transfer from Samui Airport?

We charge ฿200 plus ฿12 per km from pickup to drop-off, with a minimum of ฿300. That's ฿300 to Chaweng, Bang Rak, Choeng Mon or Bophut, about ฿380 to Lamai and about ฿490 to Nathon, one way. We confirm the exact price when you book.

How many people and bags fit in one car?

Up to 3 passengers with 3 suitcases per car. For a bigger group or more luggage, tell us when you book and we'll arrange more than one car.

What if my flight is delayed?

Give us your flight number when you book. We track the flight and adjust the pick-up time.

Can I book a transfer for my villa guests?

Yes. Villa owners and hosts can book airport pick-ups for their guests, and we can also stock groceries and clean the villa before they arrive.

Do you also take people to the airport?

Yes, drop-offs to Samui Airport cost the same as pick-ups, based on the distance from your villa or hotel.

What cars do you use?

Clean, air-conditioned cars, most of them electric, driven by our own licensed, background-checked drivers.
