    FAQ

    Good to know

    Which areas do you serve?

    We serve all of Koh Samui, including Chaweng, Bophut, Lamai, Maenam, Bang Rak, Choeng Mon, Plai Laem, Nathon, Lipa Noi and Taling Ngam. Send a request with your address and we'll confirm the details right away.

    How is pricing calculated?

    Pricing depends on the service, size of the job and duration. Home cleaning starts from ฿500 for a rental room, ฿700 for a condo and ฿1,000 for a 2-bedroom house. See the cleaning prices. We confirm an exact price with you before anything is scheduled.

    Can I combine several services on the same day?

    Yes. Pick as many services as you like in one request — your concierge will coordinate the timing.

    Do I need to be home during the service?

    Not always. For cleaning, car washing and grocery put-away, we can arrange secure access with you in advance.

    Can I set up recurring bookings?

    Absolutely. Weekly or bi-weekly cleaning, regular grocery runs and standing chauffeur schedules are all available.
